Transistir Social Tech

What it is

Transistir Social Tech is the social tech company I co-founded, dedicated to developing hardware and software with a focus on decentralized technologies. The work happens side by side with indigenous organizations, research institutes and social movements — technology made with, not for.

Current and recent projects include:

  • Kutary — mobile app for indigenous territorial data collection, with COIAB
  • CadeiaDominial — web system for land-tenure analysis over Indigenous Lands (UFPA/FADESP)
  • TerraLab — monitoring dashboard and participatory workshops with IEB
  • Guardian Connector — territorial monitoring alerts and mapping interfaces

Guiding idea

The company takes its name from the essay An Invitation to Transistence:

Not a body that opposes until it exhausts itself, but a body that lets itself be crossed in order to reconfigure the field.

Technologies

  • Free and open hardware and software
  • Offline-first and low-power systems for field work
  • Web platforms, dashboards and geodata (GeoJSON, KML, GPX)
