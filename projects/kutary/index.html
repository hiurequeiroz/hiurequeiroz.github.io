Kutary — COIAB

What it is

Kutary is a mobile application for collecting indigenous territorial data in the field, developed in partnership with the Coordination of Indigenous Organizations of the Brazilian Amazon (COIAB). Phase 1 covered technical requirements, UI/UX design and database modeling; phase 2 (ongoing) is development, customization, testing and deployment with the communities.

The app is built on top of the CoMapeo ecosystem, maintained as forks in the Transistir GitHub organization:

  • comapeo-mobile — the mobile app
  • comapeo-core — data and collaboration library
  • comapeo-schema — data schemas

Why it matters

Territorial monitoring is a daily practice of indigenous organizations. A tool designed with — and for — the people who defend the territory turns field observations into organized, shareable evidence, without depending on external platforms.

Technologies

  • CoMapeo (offline-first mobile mapping)
  • Local-first data and peer-to-peer synchronization
  • Participatory design sessions with community monitors
