Critical Reflections

Each seminar brought valuable insights and different perspectives on themes relevant to my research. Through this portfolio I document not only the presented content, but also my personal reflections and how each theme connects with my doctoral work on sociotechnical co-creation with family farmers.

Skill Development

The seminars have been fundamental for developing:

  • Critical analysis of different theoretical approaches
  • Connections between theory and practice
  • Reflection on the role of technology in society
  • A broader view of innovation and development

Research Process

This portfolio is also a record of my research process, showing how the themes and discussions of the seminars influence and enrich my doctoral work — from participatory methodologies to the politics of technology.

Academic Production

The reflections and learnings documented here feed directly into my academic production: articles, presentations and outreach — including the SEMEA-TEC extension project and sessions such as Semea-tec: seeding tech, growing autonomy at DWeb Camp 2026.

Next Steps

This portfolio is updated regularly with new reflections and learnings as new seminars and research cycles happen.
