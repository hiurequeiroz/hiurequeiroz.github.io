Transistir Social Tech

Description: The social tech company I co-founded: hardware and software built around decentralized technologies, developed together with indigenous organizations, research institutes and social movements. Current work includes territorial monitoring apps, land-tenure analysis systems for indigenous lands and community dashboards.

Kutary — COIAB

Description: Mobile app for collecting indigenous territorial data in the field, developed with the Coordination of Indigenous Organizations of the Brazilian Amazon (COIAB) on top of CoMapeo forks (mobile, core and schema), with testing and deployment alongside the communities.

Links: More Info | Source

SEMEA-TEC

Description: UNIFESP extension project co-creating low-cost, low-power social technologies with family farmers: JavAlarm (electric fence monitoring over LoRa radio) and Shitakiometer (autonomous greenhouse sensing station with solar power and real-time dashboards).

Links: More Info | Source

Coletivo Coolab

Description: A collective project I have been part of since 2016, bringing together people and community telecommunication initiatives that foster autonomous infrastructures through technical training and activation. Coolab is a community organization that develops and promotes technology for the benefit of communities through an innovative and collaborative approach. As a result, we have created several tools to support groups and organizations developing technologies for community contexts — many available on the website, alongside field activities with partner communities.
